How the prediction is made
  1. Your original answers are cleaned with the same rules as training (for example, experience longer than your age band allows is treated as blank, and tool counts are capped at 120 and 20).
  2. Engineered inputs are computed: log work experience, log company size and years of coding before professional work.
  3. The training-fitted encoder turns answers into 328 columns (one-hot and multi-hot, standardized numbers, missing flags).
  4. Fourteen L1-regularized logistic regressions (C = 0.3) give P(salary above each bucket boundary); an isotonic correction keeps them in order.
  5. Differences of these give the 15 bucket probabilities, blended 5% with the training bucket shares (λ = 0.05). The predicted bucket is the median.

Test-set accuracy: on average 1.25 buckets from the true bucket; 70% of respondents are within one bucket. Recommendations are associations in survey data, not causal effects: technologies also mark employers, roles and seniority, and “worked with” does not mean proficiency.
